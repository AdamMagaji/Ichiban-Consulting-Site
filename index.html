Where most clients start

The SQL Server health check

A fixed-price review that tells you, in plain language, whether you could recover your data tomorrow and what to fix first. We use read-only access and change nothing on your servers.

What we review

Backup and recoveryCan you restore, and how much would you lose?
SecurityWho has access, and is data encrypted?
Integrity and maintenanceCorruption checks, index and statistics jobs.
Configuration and patchingVersion support, settings and alerts.
PerformanceSlow queries, waits, memory and CPU.
CapacityDisk growth and how long you have left.

  1. Scoping call30 minutes to understand your systems and agree read-only access.
  2. ReviewFive working days. Your team keeps working; nothing is changed.
